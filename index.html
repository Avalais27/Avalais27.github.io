About

I work on computational optical imaging, with a focus on signal processing algorithms for optical coherence tomography (OCT). My current work includes spectral windowing for sidelobe suppression and statistical frameworks for speckle noise reduction.

My research sits at the intersection of Fourier optics, computational imaging, and biomedical photonics. I am especially interested in physics-informed reconstruction pipelines and robust image formation methods for biomedical imaging systems.
